2 · Mint Hero

Connect your wallet to see mint status.

Free mint: 2 per wallet, Agent ID holders only. Paid mint: 0.005 BNB/Hero, max 10 Heroes/wallet (free + paid). 100% of paid-mint revenue goes to the season pool — no dev cut. Max supply 10,000 Heroes.

Connect your wallet to see your Heroes.

4 · Merge — combine 2 Heroes

Burn 2 base Heroes (tier NONE) to receive 1 new Hero at level max(level A, level B) + 1, max Lv10. It takes 10 base Heroes to reach Lv10. Tier-forged Heroes cannot merge.

5 · Forge — forge a Lv10 Hero into a tier Hero

Only Lv10 tier-NONE Heroes can be forged. The tier is rolled randomly (based on prevrandao, not Chainlink VRF); if the rolled tier is at its cap it automatically drops to a tier with room left. Uncommon is uncapped.

The tier you receive is random — there is no way to guarantee a high tier.

6 · Battle — PvP

Note: Battling means betting real BNB. Win chance = your power / (your power + opponent’s power) — shown honestly before you accept. The winner takes 95% of the total bet + 1,000 $FORGE; the remaining 5% goes to the season pool. Heroes are never lost, win or lose — only the bet is at risk. Rewards are not guaranteed.
